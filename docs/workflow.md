# Question-driven research workflow

This is an orchestration contract for the host agent, not an installed scheduler.

1. Read the private profile and dossiers. Establish actual asset capabilities,
   purposes, dependencies, limitations and open needs from authorized sources.
   Keep private individual context out of shared publication.
2. Collect broad news feeds and primary-source signals. Balance discovery beyond
   owned products with explicit searches for unsolved household questions.
3. Choose a thesis: what changed that could unlock a previously unsuitable option?
   Connect multiple signals and older evidence. Do not equate a release with value.
4. Research the decisive unknowns using configured host web tools. Minimize private
   context in queries. Inspect primary documentation, hardware requirements,
   operating modes, measurements, total costs and alternatives as appropriate.
5. Challenge the thesis: does the household already have this capability? Would a
   simpler or non-electronic option work? What evidence would reverse the verdict?
6. Persist the dossier with claim-level provenance, fact versus inference, known
   constraints, next searches, decision and reason. Reopen when decisive evidence
   changes. `propose` is an editorial decision, never execution authorization.
7. Publish only useful synthesis: what changed, why it matters here, why the
   recommendation follows, and what concrete work the agent can take over.
   If compatibility is unresolved, research or test it rather than presenting a
   guess as a household recommendation. Be clear about remaining uncertainty.
8. Delivery and subsequent execution use separately authorized host capabilities.
   No change to models, devices or automations follows automatically from a feed.

Acceptance is an evidenced useful opportunity beyond a release summary, not
merely more articles fetched or a syntactically valid proposal. Include local AI,
assistants, speech, energy, sustainability, robotics and simple non-AI solutions
when household needs justify them; categories alone do not establish relevance.
